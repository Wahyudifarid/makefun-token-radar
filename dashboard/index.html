Token Launch Radar

Every protocol that could still launch a token, ranked by what that token would be worth.

How the radar prices a launch

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity. The category multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the live-token protocols in the same category, taken from this very snapshot — never a number typed in by hand. A category with only one or two live comparables is shrunk toward the global median, three parts global to each comparable, and a category with none is priced from its group median (yield-and-credit, trading, staking, money or real-world) or the global median. Each live ratio is capped at the snapshot’s own upper Tukey fence before any median is taken, so one extreme protocol cannot price a whole category. Growth is the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −60%..+120% and half-weighted. Maturity rises from 0.60 to 1.00 over a protocol’s first three years with no token. No protocol name is ever special-cased: the same formula scores every row.

A protocol is “pre-token” when DefiLlama records no token for it. That is the record speaking, not a promise: a listed protocol may still hold a token the record does not carry. Wrapped, staked and bridged products, exchanges and versioned pools are left out — they are not launches waiting to happen.
